# CareerLaunch Agent: Competitor and Market Research

Owner: Member 1 (Jensen). Researched 2026-11-05.

**How to read this:** these findings come from web search results, not from using the products. Many sources are vendor pages or competitor blogs. Open a source and confirm it before putting any number in the pitch.

## The main finding

The brief says our advantage is "a complete workflow instead of one isolated tool." **That is weaker than we assumed.** Simplify already gives students much of that workflow for free, and universities already pay for Handshake, Big Interview and VMock.

Being "all in one place" won't win. The better opening is **trust**: employers increasingly doubt AI-polished applications, and we can be the tool that makes a student's application provably true. See `docs/standout-ideas.md`.

## What the market evidence says

### Employers are losing trust in applications

- A 2026 study of 998 hiring leaders found [only a third are very confident résumés reflect true skills, and 92% of recruiting leaders call AI-generated résumés commonplace](https://businessjournaldaily.com/employers-losing-confidence-in-resumes-study-finds/).
- A UK Institute of Student Employers report found [around half of employers think students overstate skills using AI](https://hrreview.co.uk/hr-news/recruitment/over-half-of-employers-worried-student-use-ai-misrepresenting-skills/381944). This is UK data.
- NACE, the US career-services association, published a piece on [AI fraud and auto-apply](https://naceweb.org/career-readiness/best-practices/the-ghostwritten-candidate-ai-fraud-auto-apply-and-the-fight-for-authentic-career-readiness), saying recruiters struggle to tell real candidates from fake ones.
- **Don't quote a rejection rate.** Sources range from [about 20%](https://nrca.net/RoofingNews/Hiring%20managers%20sometimes%20reject%20applicants%20who%20use%20AI.9-16-2025.12931/Details/Story) to [80%](https://app.gem.com/blog/detect-ai-generated-resumes) and measure different things.

### Career centers care about AI, and they are stretched

- The same NACE piece cites a 2026 survey in which [55% of career centers offer workshops on AI-assisted job searching and another 20% plan to](https://naceweb.org/career-readiness/best-practices/the-ghostwritten-candidate-ai-fraud-auto-apply-and-the-fight-for-authentic-career-readiness).
- NACE's 2021-22 benchmark shows a [mean of 2,263 students per professional staff member](https://WWW.NACEWEB.ORG/uploadedFiles/files/2022/publication/executive-summary/2022-nace-career-services-benchmark-executive-summary.pdf). It is the most reliable ratio we found, but it is several years old. Vendor blogs repeat a "1 to 1,889" figure that we could not confirm, so don't use it.

### Some listings aren't worth applying to

- Estimates vary widely: [Greenhouse data suggests 18% to 22%](https://glider.ai/?p=47253) of postings are ghost jobs, while [Clarify Capital's analysis of 175,000 listings found about 1 in 7](https://clarifycapital.com/ghost-jobs-2026).
- A [LiveCareer survey of 900+ HR professionals](https://livecareer.com/resources/careers/ghost-jobs) found 45% say they regularly post ghost jobs.
- **We found no internship-specific figure.** These numbers cover jobs in general and shouldn't be presented as internship data.

## What this changes

1. **Lead with trust, not convenience.** "Your application is provably true" is harder to copy than "all in one place."
2. **Work with career centers and Handshake, not against them.** Selling a replacement for Handshake is unrealistic for a new product. Being an add-on that gives advisors a better view of a student is more believable.
3. **Expect slow sales.** University contracts appear negotiated and multi-year, so a pilot with one department or program is a more realistic first step.
4. **Check our own school first.** If it already pays for one of these tools, that shapes our entire pitch.
